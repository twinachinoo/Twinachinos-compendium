# this is the everything book.
Welcome, travellers from afar. Thank you for playing in a twinachino campaign. Below is content that is used in all of my campaigns, though not all of it will be made avaliable at once. Consider this a compendium for all my homebrew.

### usage of custom features
The usage of anything within this book depends entirely on an agreement between player and Dm.

#### New conditions
New conditions are introduced to offer more variety to gameplay and help buff some underrated abilities or spells as they do.

#### new feats
New feats serve to offer more diverse build paths, particularly for martials, other than just taking raw ASIs. They are all generally quite strong. Additionally, two feats have been made avaliable to the warlock at level 8, where they can sacrifice their ASI to gain an additional spell slot or eldritch blast beam. This is my preferred implementation of these two highly requested features.

#### new invocations
alongside gaining two new feat options, warlocks additionally gain new invocations to push a stronger eldritch blast damage build option, without relying on a subclass.

#### consumables for spells and spell slots
the introduction of runes and mana potions offers a spellcaster significantly more leeway with their builds, whilst still preserving an element of dice RNG. This means a spellcasting class can go on longer even without long rest spam.

\column

#### new spells

Spells introduced here are generally designed to suit the campaign's theme as unique abilities. They **do not have to be cast as spells**. Consider these unique abilities that creatures, traps, or others can utilize. Currently, the *artificer, bard, cleric, druid, ranger, sorcerer, warlock and wizard all gain at least one additional spell. More will be added as time goes on.* 

##### core design principle
Many of these ar designed to give more content for underrepresented damage types, effects, or classes. Consider the *artificer*, for example, would be a fantastic fit to introduce more **lightning damage**, and electricity-themed spells. Or for example, the deeply underrepresented *acid* damage type in spells.

#### new class features

New class features serve more to *patch weaknesses* than to pump more power into a class. D&D is not a balanced game; let's accept that early, and look to ensure a class's mechanical chassis does not get in the way of roleplay, storytelling, and combat.

##### multiple options
Many different house rules have been drawn from different campaigns, and ultimately, depend on an agreement between DM and player. If a class has multiple unique options, it is generally advised you only select only 1-2 to implement.

##### Optimizations
The design behind these features primarily focus on raising a class's baseline, not pushing their ceiling. This is to aid "subpar" builds still feel strong and capable. However, Optimizers might discover combinations I am not aware of. 
\page

# new conditions
Some new abilities, spells, or feats will reference new content. If you see a new term not in D&D's base conditions, refer to here.

### spell (and other) buffs
These effects help to introduce some more variety into otherwise "subpar" spell selections, such as infamous options like *acid arrow* or *hellish rebuke* - awfully mediocre options for the spell slot, now compete based on unique effects instead of damage.
:
other items and class abilities might be affected by these conditions, but I'm not too sure yet. 

#### affected spells (buffs)
Each new condition or effect will come with a section like this to detail which spells have been granted with new bonuses.

### coated with acid
This effect becomes permenant against a *construct*-type creature, or at least, until they are targeted by a *lesser* restoration or greater healing spell.
:
**coated with acid:** If the creature wears metal armor, they gain a -1 penalty to AC granted by the armor. If the creature wields metal weapons, they gain a -1 penalty to attack and damage rolls. 
:
**coated with vicious acid:** If the creature wears metal armor, they gain a -2 penalty to AC granted by the armor. If the creature wields metal weapons, they gain a -2 penalty to attack and damage rolls with it. 

#### affected spells (buffs)
*acid arrow* now also inflicts *coated with acid* on a failed save.
:
*vitriolic sphere* now also inflicts *coated with vicious acid* on a failed save.

### off balance
Seeing as strength-based melee martials can get screwed over a million different ways with conditions, this condition gives characters an oppertunity to screw over a rogue too. 
:
**off balance**: The affected creature has disadvantage on dexterity checks and saving throws, and loses the bonus granted by dexterity to their AC. A creature recovers from being off balance at the start of their next turn.

\column
## class specific conditions

### shadowflame
A unique variant of the burning condition avaliable to the warlock, it progressively grows in power as you gain levels, and cannot be put out with water. It can still be put out by the conventional method of spending your action to drop prone, to roll around and end the burning condition.
:
**shadowflame**: When a creature is burning with shadowflame, on the start of each of its turns, it takes necrotic damage equal to **1d4+half your warlock level**. This means it scales into late game with relevancy, compared to the meager 1d4 of burning.

#### affected spells (buffs)
Creatures that fail the save against *arms of hadar* or *hellish rebuke* also causes the target to *begin burning with shadowflame* on a failed save.

### Hollow projection
Unique and powerful buff spell options avaliable exclusively to the artificer. This effectively lets them put up an additional "infusion effect" for the whole party, as the spells are non-concentration and last for 8 whole hours; it comes at the cost of a high level spell slot.
:
For balance reasons, only a single hollow projection can exist at one time.

### brimstone flames
a variant of the burning condition avaliable to arcane casters. Brimstone flames cannot be put out save for magical means, and it disables fire resistance and immunities. Think of it as an extra evil, hellish fire that even creatures of fire can't stand.
:
**brimstone flames**: When a creature is burning with brimstone flames, at the start of each of its turns, it takes 3d4 fire damage as blue hellish flame burns your soul. If it has resistance or immunity to fire damage, **that resistance or immunity is removed until the condition ends**.

## magical consumables  
Additional ways to learn spells or regain spell slots.


### mana potions
*Potion, Rarity Varies*
:
A liquid that swirls with shades of blue, with a flourescent sheen. This potion is a magic item. As a Bonus Action, you can drink it and regain a number of spell slots, which have a combined level that is equal to or less than 1d4 worth of spell levels. You cannot drink another mana potion until you finish a short or long rest. 
:
If you drink the potion as an action, you forgo the dice roll and instead gain a set number of spell levels equal to the dice average (round up). 
:
The number of spell slots you can restore depends on the potion’s rarity, as shown in the table below.

### runes
*wonderous item, common*
:
A shimmering gylph of interlocking geometric shapes, that glows with iridescent colors and hums with arcane energy. The glyph contains a random spell of a certain spell level from a certain class. 
:
Over the course of one hour, you can infuse the rune into your body, in which it imprints itself into your skin. When you do, you learn a random spell from a class's spell list, the level of which is determined by the rune's rarity, and the class is determined by the rune type, both shown in the tables below. 
:
once infused, you can cast this spell for free, and can't do so again until you finish a long rest. When the spell is cast, the rune loses it's radiance, and regains it after a long rest. 
:
You can also cast this spell with any spell slots you have, and it does not count against the number of spells you know. If you have the *spellcasting* or *pact magic* feature, you can use your spell save DC and attack bonus in place of the rune's.

*1st level transmutation (Ranger)*

**Casting Time:** :: Bonus Action
**Range:**        :: Self
**Components:**   :: S
**Duration:**     :: 1 minute

You suffuse an piece of ammunition with the elemental energy of air that can create a rushing current of air to knock creatures prone.
:
When you hit a creature with an attack roll using this ammunition, In addition to the normal effect of the attack, the creature takes 1d8 thunder damage, and is knocked prone.

#### Aquatic arrow
*1st level transmutation (Ranger)* 

**Casting Time:** :: Bonus Action
**Range:**        :: Self
**Components:**   :: S
**Duration:**     :: 1 minute

You suffuse an piece of ammunition with the elemental energy of water that can freeze creatures in place.
:
When you hit a creature with an attack roll using this ammunition within, In addition to the normal effect of the attack, they take 1d8 cold damage, and must make a strength saving throw or have their speed reduced to 0 and become *vunerable* to lightning damage until the end of your next turn.

#### arcane arrow
*1st level transmutation (Ranger, artificer)*

**Casting Time:** :: Bonus Action
**Range:**        :: Self
**Components:**   :: S
**Duration:**     :: 8 hours

You suffuse up to four pieces of ammunition with magical potency, allowing them to seek out targets. For the duration of the spell, they become arcane arrows, which gain a +1 to attack and damage rolls. When you miss with an arcane arrow, you can use your reaction to reroll the attack against a different target within 30 feet.
:
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the increase to attack and damage rolls increases to by one, and you select an additional four pieces of ammunition for each slot level above 2nd.

*2nd level transmutation (Ranger)*

**Casting Time:** :: Bonus Action
**Range:**        :: Self
**Components:**   :: S
**Duration:**     :: 1 minute

You suffuse an piece of ammunition with the elemental energy of acid that eats away at metal equipment.
:
When you hit a creature with an attack roll using this ammunition, In addition to the normal effect of the attack, they must make a Constitution saving throw, or take 2d8 acid damage, and become *coated with acid*, until the acid is washed off or the spell ends.

*2nd level transmutation (Ranger, artificer)*

**Casting Time:** :: Bonus Action
**Range:**        :: Self
**Components:**   :: S
**Duration:**     :: 1 minute

You suffuse an piece of ammunition with the elemental energy of fire that erupts into a blossom of flames.
:

On a failed save, they take 3d8 fire damage, and begin burning. On a successful save, they take half damage, and aren't affected by burning.

**Casting Time:** :: Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a target with an ranged attack roll.
**Range:**        :: Self
**Components:**   :: S, V

As your attack strikes the target, you transmute it into a coiling serpent that pulls creatures together.
:
In addition to the normal effect of the attack, they take 1d8 poison damage, and must make a strength saving throw, or be pulled up to 15ft towards you. Alternatively, you forgo the save and pull yourself 15ft towards them.

## druid spells

#### acidic exhalation 
*2nd level evocation (Druid)*

**Casting Time:** :: Action
**Range:**        :: 30ft (cone)
**Components:**   :: V
**Duration:**     :: 1 minute (acid duration)
Your breath is infused with the elemental energy of acid. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a Constitution saving throw. 
:
On a failed save, they take 6d4 acid damage, and become *coated with acid*, until the acid is washed off or the spell ends. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t coated with acid.
:
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 2d4 for each slot level above 2nd.

#### Mote of moonlight 
*4th level conjuration (Druid, cleric)*

**Casting Time:** :: Action
**Range:**        :: 300ft, under open sky
**Components:**   :: V, S, M
**Duration:**     :: instantaneous

You call down a silver pillar of moonlight that descends in a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on a point within range. All creatures inside the cylinder must make a constitution saving throw or take 6d8 radiant damage, and are blinded. On a successful save, they take half damage, and are not blinded.
:

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.

*2nd level conjuration (ranger, druid)*

**Casting Time:** :: 1 Action
**Range:**        :: 60ft
**Components:**   :: S, M
**Duration:**     :: 10 minutes (wind ball duration)

:
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the vortex's radius increases by 10ft for each slot level above 1st.

#### primal shell 
*3rd level abjuration (ranger, druid)*

**Casting Time:** :: 1 bonus action
**Range:**        :: touch
**Components:**   :: V, S, M
**Duration:**     :: 8 hours

:
Alternatively, you touch a or a *wildshaped* or *polymorphed* creature. The creature's AC cannot be lower than 18, and the amount of temporary hitpoints they gain by being *wildshaped* or *polymorphed* increases by 8.

## bard spells

#### encore
*1st level enchantment (bard)*

**Casting Time:** :: 1 reaction, which you take when you or a creature you can see within range makes a weapon attack roll as an action.
**Range:**        :: 30ft
**Components:**   :: V
**Duration:**     :: Instantaneous
If the selected creature can hear you, it can immediately make one weapon attack with the same weapon as part of the same action. This attack follows the weapon’s normal restrictions, including the loading property. If the attack hits, it deals an extra 1d6 thunder damage.
:
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the extra thunder damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.

#### Symphonic shield
*4th level abjuration (bard)*

**Casting Time:** :: 1 action
**Range:**        :: self (20ft radius)
**Components:**   :: V
**Duration:**     :: concentration, up to 1 minute
Waves of sonic energy reverbate from you, repelling incoming attacks, and protecting you and creatures of your choice within a 20ft sphere centered on you that moves with you. 
:
Protected creatures gain a +2 bonus to their AC against ranged attack rolls, a +2 bonus to all dexterity saving throws, and take 2 less damage from all weapon or spell attacks made against them. A projectile from a ranged attack that misses is deflected back to the attacking creature using the same attack roll.
:
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the AC bonus, dexterity bonus, and damage reduction increase by 1 for each slot level above 1st.

\column

*3rd level transmutation (bard)*

**Range:**        :: touch
**Components:**   :: V, S
**Duration:**     :: concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch a melee weapon, and set the metal to reverbate and oscillate rapidly. For the duration, the weapon has a +1 bonus to attack rolls and deals an extra 1d4 thunder damage when it hits.
:
Once per turn, when a creature is hit with this weapon, in addition to the normal effect of the attack, they must make a constitution saving throw or be thrown *off balance*.

# martials like good feats
Title says it all. Sharpshooter, great weapon master, polearm master. It often does feel like these are mandatory in order to make a build work. But when I play a class that is meant to be good at dealing damage, I want to deal a lot of damage, And do it a way I enjoy. These feats are not going to be as intensely powerful as GWM or PM, but should still offer strength and open new build paths.

### feat scaling?
All feats are just suddenly unlocked by level 4. What about making more powerful feats gated behind higher levels? You know - basic progression? There will be some soft requirements in place here. 

#### proficiency with martial weapons
A simple yet important gate for certain powerful feats, which should not be avaliable to already powerful blaster builds. It does make them avaliable to gishes, though. I'm a swords bard fan.

#### feat at level 6 for rogues
I would highly reccomend using the optional house rule where the rogue gains an additional ASI at level 6, if you wish to fully utilize the power of these feats. This is a noticeable power increase to the class. (see: chapter 3: house rules)

# new class features
These are a collection of house rules gathered over time playing with multiple different groups, where we adressed some common sore points, whether it be in 2014 or 2024. Ultimately, the impelementation of these rules depends on the DM's discretion. 


## design commentary
### warlock
2024 made warlock less centered on EB, but it's still **the** damaging cantrip for most builds. Rather awful for the "tax" on your invocations and cantrip slots to something the class is known for, even if it's iconic. 
:
The new beshadowed blast feature is much more impactful on a 2014 warlock than a 2024 warlock, as the latter learns more invocations and spells. 

### bard
These are very much campaign specific house rules, due to the length of time between long rests in my campaigns, bard simply replenishes all their bardic inspiration on a short rest, and regains the song of rest feature. These features mean the bard can still use their signiature abilities between long rests, and they actually *help* recovery during short rests with SoR.
:
Don't kill me. I know swords bard exists.

### fighter
:
Fighter's great in 24. The champion is still considered the worst subclass, though, so their buff buff is mainly aimed at helping weaker builds just...do more damage - PB damage once per turn doesn't help the sweaty crossbow expert a lot, but it does make sword and board feel a bit nicer. 

#### master of arms
The implementation of adding maneuvers and superiority dice into the base class is a common house rule for the fighter. This is a noticeable power boost, so I've chosen to grant the dice and maneuver gains at important "spike" levels - 5, 9, 13, 17, mirroring half caster progression. This also means they gain a direct damaging feature on levels 9 and 13.


\column

### ranger
Ranger's *favoured foe* has been updated to incorporate the new *arcane arrow*, and the new elemental quiver feature adds and *elemental arrow* spells into the base kit, so you can do something with this additional class resource outside of casting hunter's mark....again.

### rogue
**Implementing all the new features here will break balance.** I reccomend the level 6 optional ASI + 1 combat feature. In addition, I would not reccomend using the crit rate boost at level 5 if the custom feats are enabled; there are crit rate granting feats, and crit rate is a steep exponential damage increase to unbalanced levels.
:
There's a lot to say about rogue, and that's because it's maybe my favourite class. Rogue damage is considered fine for T(ier) 1 play, levels 1-4. T2 is when they really begin to fall off without extra attack. Are any of these new features individually as good as extra attack? No. I don't think rogues should get Extra attack, and their current 2024 identity still focuses on a single, precise strike. 

#### ASI at level 6
This is just an additional ASI, but it's impact is immense - early 20 dex, elven accuracy, etc. I've noticed some rogue players like to go all in on damage; even if not, other rogues may not want a second set of expertises at level 6 and prefer completing their build early. Expertise at level 9 the same as bard and ranger in 2024.
#### critcality
Criticality is a unique custom option, giving the class some on-demand nova capacity, but it moves away from the idea of the consistent martial striker they were in 2014. Mathematically, it comes out to about the same as d8 sneak attack - it's about giving more options over just doing strictly more damage.

# the psion of 2024

I'm not the biggest fan of 2024 UA psion. Their multiple-scaling dice mechanic is cool, but ultimately, they still feel too similar to their other d6 caster counterparts - sorcerer and wizard. Whatever the case, the UA psion is a class that is allowed to be used in this campaign, but I am still considering alternative implementations of this class that are better designed and more mechanically distinct.

## class features
The 2024 psion's class features are a little all over the place, but it's entirely rescuable. Certain features will be tweaked slightly, and additional small features will be added, to make class progression more satisfying. The psion has very poor resource economy, the class is constantly starved for resources - having to spend multiple resources just to achieve one outcome. As a psion gains levels, certain psionic techniques should be cognitively trivial for a master of the mind.
:
#### psionic restoration
Reworked to be the psion equivalent of the 2024 monk’s Focus recovery: it restores only expended Psionic Energy Dice, not spell slots. It should come online early, at level 2. A small amount of healing also helps the class’s d6 hit dice.

#### psionic power
Psions are starved for their psionic energy die. Tying their level 1 core features to a random roll on a dice - one of your very few dice, by the way, seems like too many steps for little reward. Telepathy has been standrdized to level x 10, *for free*, and telekinetic propel is enhanced so it can be used as an action or bonus action, and the dice roll's push now scales with your PB.

\column

### subclass features
The psion's subclasses are designed...very awkwardly. They often design features around needing to consume spell slots and psionic energy die - which is a full subclass feature, a spell slot, and a psionic energy die, to do one thing. Again, resource cost and economy.
#### psykinetic: level 6: rebounding shield
A 6th level subclass feature built on a 1st level spell. This is now a **3rd level subclass feature**, and they recieve a new feature at level 6 - **Psionic siege**, that allows them to break objects (notoriously hard with psychic damage) or bypass psychic damage resistance, increasing their DPR indirectly.

#### psykinetic: level 10: enhanced telekinetic crush
This is a 10th level feature that enhances a middling 3rd level spell. It's not great. This feature now grants them the ability to cast it once per long rest.

#### telepath: level 3: mind infiltrator
This costs a full 2nd level spell slot and a psionic energy die. And that's the entire subclass feature. Are we noticing a pattern? detect thoughts is a great spell, but a subclass dedicated to telepathy should be able to do it at little cost.

#### metamorph: level 3: Psychic shell
This is just draconic sorcerer's unarmored defense feature, on a subclass that really, really needs defenses. I've chosen to omit giving them hitpoints as they gain temporary ones via their other class features and spells.

### new spells (psionic spells)
With many of these campaign settings including users of psionics, these are some additional abilities avaliable to them. Currently, these are made to resemble spells, but may be moved into their own system later, depending on which iteration of the psion or mystic class is being used during a campaign.

#### combat capabilities
Most of these are options designed to enhance their combat capabilities. They are quite powerful, offering good damage and strong control, but they never get a fireball-level of raw power. They should get something in their psionic niche, like disrupting concentration, exploiting the weak *intelligence* save, or inflicting madness.
